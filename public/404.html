Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This policy explains what information C&N Spotless collects through this website and how we use it. We keep it simple: we only collect what we need to answer your quote request.

Information you give us

When you send the quote form, we receive the details you enter: your name, phone number, email address, vehicle or property type, the service you need, your preferred date, your area or ZIP code, and any message you write. If you call, text or email us, we receive your phone number or email address and what you send.

How we use it

  • To reply to your request, give you a quote and schedule the work.
  • To contact you about a job you've booked with us.

We do not sell, rent or trade your information, and we do not use it for advertising or add you to a mailing list.

Services that help run this site

A few outside services are used to run this website. Each handles data under its own privacy policy.

  • Formspree delivers the quote form to our email inbox. Your form details pass through Formspree to reach us.
  • Google Maps shows our service-area map. Google may receive your IP address and set its own cookies when the map loads.
  • Google Fonts provides the text fonts on this site, which means Google receives your IP address when the page loads.
  • Elfsight displays our Google reviews. It loads from Elfsight's servers and may receive your IP address.
  • Google Analytics and Google Ads (the Google tag) measure how visitors find and use this site, such as which pages are viewed and whether the quote form was sent or the phone number tapped, so we can see which ads bring in customers. Google sets cookies to do this and handles the data under its own privacy policy.
  • Vercel hosts this website and keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, browser and pages requested) for security and reliability.

Cookies and tracking

We use the Google tag (Google Analytics and Google Ads), which sets cookies to measure visits and to tell us when an ad led to a quote request or a call. Google Maps and Elfsight may also set their own cookies. You can limit ad personalization at adssettings.google.com, opt out of Google Analytics with Google's opt-out browser add-on, or block cookies in your browser settings. To limit repeat spam, the quote form keeps a short note in your own browser of when it was last used; it contains no personal details and never leaves your device.

How long we keep it

We keep quote requests and customer contact details only as long as we need them to serve you and keep normal business records. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing us. We'll respond as soon as we reasonably can.

Children

This website is meant for adults arranging window tinting services. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it on this page and change the date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your information: email clayontrowers6@gmail.com or call (954) 213-7808.

Terms of Service

Last updated:

Quotes and scheduling

Quotes given through this site are estimates based on the information provided. Final pricing is confirmed once we see the vehicle or windows. No work is scheduled until both sides confirm a date.

Mobile service conditions

As a mobile service, we need a suitable place to work: reasonably clean, sheltered from wind, rain and direct debris, with enough room around the vehicle or windows. If conditions on arrival make a quality install impossible, we will reschedule rather than do substandard work.

Tint laws

Window tint limits are set by state law and vary by window position. We will advise on legal limits to the best of our knowledge, but the vehicle owner is responsible for compliance in their jurisdiction.

Cure time and aftercare

  • Keep windows up for 3 to 5 days after installation.
  • Haze and small water pockets during curing are normal and are not defects.
  • Clean with ammonia-free products only. Ammonia damages film and may void warranty coverage.

Warranty

Film warranties are provided by the film manufacturer and vary by product. The applicable warranty for your film will be identified before installation.
