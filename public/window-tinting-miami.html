Miami window tinting without the shop visit.

Getting to a tint shop in Miami traffic can eat half a day. C&N Spotless comes to you instead: your building's garage, your driveway or your office lot. We offer car window tinting, home window film and commercial tinting across Miami and the rest of Miami-Dade.

High-rise and condo garages

A covered parking garage is one of the best places to tint a car, since it keeps wind and dust off the glass. If you live or work in a high-rise, we can usually do the install right in your space. Check that your building allows contractors to work in the garage, and let us know about any access or parking passes.

Heat, glare and privacy for your car

Miami sun is strong year-round. Ceramic and carbon films reject far more infrared heat than standard dyed films, block nearly all UV, and add privacy on the rear windows. We'll recommend the darkest shade that keeps your vehicle legal under Florida law.

Condos and homes

Floor-to-ceiling glass lets in a lot of heat and glare. Residential film cuts both and protects floors and furniture from fading. Most condo associations have rules about how film looks from outside, so check with yours first; we'll provide samples and spec sheets for approval.

Storefronts and offices

For shops and offices, we work around your hours, before opening, after close or on weekends, so business carries on as normal.

Florida tint limits

Front side windows must let in at least 28% of light. Rear side and back windows can go to 15% on cars or 6% on SUVs, trucks and vans. See the full table on our automotive tint page.

Quotes and scheduling

Quotes given through this site are estimates based on the information provided. Final pricing is confirmed once we see the vehicle or windows. No work is scheduled until both sides confirm a date.

Mobile service conditions

As a mobile service, we need a suitable place to work: reasonably clean, sheltered from wind, rain and direct debris, with enough room around the vehicle or windows. If conditions on arrival make a quality install impossible, we will reschedule rather than do substandard work.

Tint laws

Window tint limits are set by state law and vary by window position. We will advise on legal limits to the best of our knowledge, but the vehicle owner is responsible for compliance in their jurisdiction.

Cure time and aftercare

  • Keep windows up for 3 to 5 days after installation.
  • Haze and small water pockets during curing are normal and are not defects.
  • Clean with ammonia-free products only. Ammonia damages film and may void warranty coverage.

Warranty

Film warranties are provided by the film manufacturer and vary by product. The applicable warranty for your film will be identified before installation.
