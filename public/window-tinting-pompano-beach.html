Coastal window tinting, brought to your door.

Pompano Beach gets strong sun and a lot of reflected glare off the ocean, the Intracoastal and the canals. C&N Spotless is a mobile service, so we come to your condo, home or business with everything we need and install the film right where the glass is.

Beachside condos

Oceanfront and Intracoastal condos tend to have large sliding doors and wide windows facing the water, which let in a lot of heat and glare in the afternoon. Window film cuts both and helps protect floors and furniture from fading. Most condo associations have rules about how film looks from outside, so check with yours first; we'll provide samples and spec sheets for approval.

Canal and waterfront homes

Water reflects sunlight back up into the house, so rooms facing a canal can be bright and hot even with blinds closed. A heat-rejecting film lets you keep the view while cutting the glare that bounces off the water.

Cars, trucks and work fleets

We tint daily drivers in your driveway or building garage, and we can tint work trucks and vans at your business lot so every vehicle in the fleet matches and stays within Florida's legal limits.

Quotes and scheduling

Quotes given through this site are estimates based on the information provided. Final pricing is confirmed once we see the vehicle or windows. No work is scheduled until both sides confirm a date.

Mobile service conditions

As a mobile service, we need a suitable place to work: reasonably clean, sheltered from wind, rain and direct debris, with enough room around the vehicle or windows. If conditions on arrival make a quality install impossible, we will reschedule rather than do substandard work.

Tint laws

Window tint limits are set by state law and vary by window position. We will advise on legal limits to the best of our knowledge, but the vehicle owner is responsible for compliance in their jurisdiction.

Cure time and aftercare

  • Keep windows up for 3 to 5 days after installation.
  • Haze and small water pockets during curing are normal and are not defects.
  • Clean with ammonia-free products only. Ammonia damages film and may void warranty coverage.

Warranty

Film warranties are provided by the film manufacturer and vary by product. The applicable warranty for your film will be identified before installation.
