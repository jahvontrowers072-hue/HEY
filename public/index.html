On The Glass

Give it a few days to cure.

Fresh film can look slightly hazy or show small water pockets for the first few days. That's moisture drying out behind the film, and it clears on its own. Leave the windows up for 3 to 5 days and skip the car wash until it's settled.

Shades are described by VLT (visible light transmission). Common options run 70%, 50%, 35%, 20% and 5%, where a lower number is darker. 35% is the most popular front-window choice; 20% and 5% are usually rear glass. Use the shade preview above to compare them side by side.

Yes, though how much depends entirely on the film rather than how dark it looks. Ceramic and infrared-rejecting films block significantly more heat than standard dyed film at the same shade. We'll show you the manufacturer's heat-rejection specs for whatever film you're considering so you're comparing real numbers.

Quality window films are rated to block up to 99% of UV rays, including clear films that barely change how the glass looks. That's what protects your interior from fading and cracking, and your skin on long drives or in a sunny room.

Under Florida law, front side windows must let at least 28% of light through. On sedans and coupes, the rear side windows and back window must let at least 15% through; on SUVs, trucks and vans those rear windows can go as dark as 6%. The windshield can only have a non-reflective strip above the AS-1 line marked on the glass, and film can't be too mirror-like (no more than 25% reflective on the front sides, 35% on the rear). Drivers with certain medical conditions can apply to the Florida DHSMV for an exemption. We'll recommend the darkest legal option for your vehicle, or tell you plainly if what you're asking for isn't legal. Limits can change, so we confirm the current rules for your vehicle before we install.

Terms of Service

Last updated:

Quotes and scheduling

Quotes given through this site are estimates based on the information provided. Final pricing is confirmed once we see the vehicle or windows. No work is scheduled until both sides confirm a date.

Mobile service conditions

As a mobile service, we need a suitable place to work: reasonably clean, sheltered from wind, rain and direct debris, with enough room around the vehicle or windows. If conditions on arrival make a quality install impossible, we will reschedule rather than do substandard work.

Tint laws

Window tint limits are set by state law and vary by window position. We will advise on legal limits to the best of our knowledge, but the vehicle owner is responsible for compliance in their jurisdiction.

Cure time and aftercare

  • Keep windows up for 3 to 5 days after installation.
  • Haze and small water pockets during curing are normal and are not defects.
  • Clean with ammonia-free products only. Ammonia damages film and may void warranty coverage.

Warranty

Film warranties are provided by the film manufacturer and vary by product. The applicable warranty for your film will be identified before installation.
